HammerOverlay

Turn “tomorrow at 5 pm” into a Discord timestamp from a small desktop overlay.

Open the overlay with your global shortcut, type a time or choose one on the calendar, and copy the format you want. Discord displays the timestamp in each reader’s time zone.

Basic parsing and the calendar work without an account. Sign in with Google to use the hosted fallback when the local parser can’t resolve an expression while the hosted model is warming up.
